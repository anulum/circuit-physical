# Roadmap

## Current foundation

**Being qualified:**
- repository identity and legal texts;
- architecture and governance;
- native documentation;
- dedicated validation tools.

**Domain capabilities and claims:** empty.

## Capability milestones

1. **The three planned v1 record schemas with golden fixtures** (`physical_operation`, `physical_result`, `shuttle_top_harness_spec`).
2. **Separate-process `OpenROAD`, `OpenSTA`, `Magic`, `KLayout` and `Netgen` adapters** enforcing post-dummy-fill extraction ordering on open PDKs.
3. **Shuttle wrapper (`Caravel`/frame), macro power-pin, `GDSII` prefix, ESD/latch-up and backside-substrate verifiers.**
4. **Migration of generic ASIC physical-flow modules** from SC-NeuroCore under split gates.

**Enrolled with every implementation change:**
- strict native typing and linting;
- native documentation;
- meaningful statement and branch coverage;
- affected boundary tests and benchmarks.

**Requires its own evidence and explicit approval:**
- dual person-in-role foundry DRC waivers;
- admitting proprietary foundry sign-off decks;
- public activation and release.

No availability date is promised. No performance, yield or productivity gain is promised either.
